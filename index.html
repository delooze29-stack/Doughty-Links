Instructions and info

  • Aim: drag the ring, or swipe the course left and right. Swipe up and down to change distance.
  • Power: grab your ball and pull it down. Past 100% is extra power, but the green zone shrinks. While you pull, move left or right to fine-tune your aim (or use the arrow keys).
  • Accuracy: let go when the needle is in the green. The harder you pull, the faster it swings. A yellow marker on the course shows how far that power will send the ball, before wind and slopes.
  • Spin: tap the ball bottom-left. Top for topspin, bottom for backspin, left side to draw and right side to fade.
  • Green view: on approach shots, tap Green view (or press G) for a close-up of the green. The yellow dots show how the ball should roll.
  • Slopes and wind: the ball rolls with the ground, so read the grid and drifting dots on the greens. The flag, trees and dust show the wind.
Good to know
  • Courses: Kingfisher Links, The Master Course, Heather Ridge, Little Wren pitch and putt, Downtown, the Train Course and Brick Park, plus a driving range and practice for any single hole.
  • Members: your name and password let you play as yourself on any device. Tap "change" on the menu to switch player.
  • Records: under the hole name you can see the best and worst score anyone has made on that hole.
  • Replays: tap Replay last shot, then the Camera button to change the angle.
  • Build your own: the Course editor lets you draw holes, test them and publish them for everyone under Community courses.
